# Disruption Risk Intelligence

A **derived indicator**, shown only on `/risk` and labelled everywhere as such. It is not a
reported event, not a confirmed delay, and not a prediction for any specific flight.

## 1. Rules baseline (`rules-v1`, always served)

Implemented in `services/api/app/services/risk.py`; recomputed every 10 minutes by the worker
(`risk` job) and persisted in `risk_scores` with every component, the inputs' timestamps and
the method version.

| Component | Points | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| FAA reported state | 0–45 | FAA NAS Status | ground stop / GDP / full closure = 45; reported arrival/departure delays = 25 |
| Observed weather | 0–25 | METAR | observed weather-risk tier: severe 25, high 18, elevated 8, low 0 |
| Forecast, next 6 h | 0–20 | TAF | worst period LIFR 15 / IFR 10; thunderstorm +10; gusts ≥ 35 kt +8 (≥ 25 kt +4); capped at 20 |
| Historical baseline | 0–15 | BTS | `clamp((rate − 0.15) × 60, 0, 15)` where rate = departures delayed ≥ 15 min or cancelled ÷ scheduled, for this airport, calendar month and scheduled local hour (≥ 30 departures required) |

Score = sum, capped at 100. Categories: **low** < 20 ≤ **moderate** < 40 ≤ **elevated** < 60 ≤ **high**.

Design properties (unit-tested in `tests/test_risk.py`):

* Weather and history alone cannot exceed 60 — "high" requires every non-FAA factor at its
  maximum, or an FAA-reported program.
* Missing inputs score 0 and are listed in `missing_inputs`; nothing is imputed.
* The forecast component only considers TAF periods overlapping the next 6 hours.

## 2. Predictive model (served only if validated)

`pipelines/historical/train_risk_model.py` investigates whether observed weather adds
predictive power beyond an airport's historical base rate.

* **Label:** an airport × local date × scheduled departure hour (≥ 5 departures) is
  *disrupted* when (delayed ≥ 15 min + cancelled) ÷ scheduled ≥ 0.35 (BTS, real outcomes).
* **Features (no leakage):** the last METAR at or before the start of the hour (≤ 75 min old)
  from the IEM ASOS archive — visibility/ceiling flags, wind, gust, thunderstorm,
  snow/freezing, rain; cyclic hour and month; weekend; airport base rate computed on the
  training period only.
* **Validation:** chronological split — the last 6 loaded months are held out. Compared
  models: base-rate+hour logistic baseline, full logistic regression (served, explainable),
  gradient boosting (reference).
* **Serving rule:** the logistic model is served next to the rules score only if its test
  ROC AUC beats the baseline by ≥ 0.02. Its output is a probability for the *airport-hour*,
  with per-feature logit contributions. Results are in
  [`risk-model-report.md`](risk-model-report.md), generated by the script.

Limitations: no historical FAA advisory archive (programs are not features); TAFs are not in
the training features; BTS covers reporting carriers' scheduled domestic flights only; an
association learned from 2024–2026 data may not hold under different operations.
